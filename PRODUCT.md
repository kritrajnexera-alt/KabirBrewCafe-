# KabirBrewCafe

## Register

brand

## Users

Local customers in Netrang and surrounding towns — people looking for a quality pizza, a quiet place for coffee, or a relaxed evening with margaritas in Jin Bazar. Also delivery/takeaway customers who order by phone. Primarily walk-in, some planned visits for group gatherings. State of mind: casual, hungry, looking for something genuine — not a chain experience.

## Product Purpose

Drive real-world visits, calls, and orders to KabirBrewCafe. The site exists to make people feel the warmth of the place before they walk through the door — the wood-fired oven, the quiet courtyard, the reliably good pizza. Success means more phone orders, more walk-ins, more regulars.

## Brand Personality

Warm, genuine, unpretentious. The voice of a cafe owner who takes pride in the pizza base and the coffee beans but doesn't use fancy words for it. Three words: **earthy, confident, hospitable**.

## Anti-references

- Generic restaurant templates (Wix/WordPress cafe themes, Bootstrap restaurant templates)
- Over-designed "artisan" sites with too much texture and not enough substance
- Corporate chain restaurant sites (Domino's, Pizza Hut — too transactional)
- Any site that uses stock photography of people laughing with salads
- "Modern minimalist" agency sites that forget they're selling pizza, not design services

## Design Principles

1. **Content over decoration.** Every visual choice serves the food, the place, the people. No decorative elements that don't trace back to the brand.
2. **Feel the room before you see it.** The site should transmit the atmosphere — warm light, wood fire, quiet evenings — through color, spacing, and texture, not just photos.
3. **One voice, everywhere.** The copy reads like a real person in Jin Bazar wrote it — specific, local, slightly informal. No marketing-speak.
4. **Generous by default.** Premium spacing, airy layouts, room to breathe. The opposite of a cramped menu board.
5. **Built for the regular, not the tourist.** Content rewards repeat visits — the full menu, the real reviews, the actual location details. This is a tool for people who already want to come.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum for all text. Large text (headlines, CTAs) targets 3:1+. Body text targets 4.5:1+ with margin. Keyboard-navigable throughout. Reduced motion respected.
